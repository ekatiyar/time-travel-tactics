UI design space for the bootstrap prototype

Eighteen mockup pages exploring how the play screen could change, from small layout moves to a different picture of time. Every page draws the same engine-generated position so the ideas can be compared: meta-turn 17 of a 16x9 bootstrap match, you are Coral holding the key and walking backward, and Amber's older grab will take it from you in 4 turns.

Mockups only, no implementationFixture from the real engine (shared/make-fixture.ts)Dark and light via the toggle

How to read these

  • Each page states the idea, shows 2 to 4 mockups with numbered callouts, then lists trade-offs, which design-doc UI requirements or open questions it answers, and a rough cost (S, M, L) split between view-only work and engine view changes.
  • Pages are grouped by axis: layout (where things go), time (how history and fronts read), board (how the board itself is drawn), flow (setup, input, learning, review), and style.
  • Most ideas combine. Suggested bundles are at the bottom.

Suggested bundles

A. Evolve what exists (M total)

01 board-first layout + 02 timeline instrument + 06 key and front language + 08 on-board input + 09 inspector. Keeps the flat board, fixes the three hardest reads (direction, key custody, front ETA), and removes the two range sliders. All view work in ui.tsx and style.css plus one small addition to the bootstrap view.

B. Show the rules moving (M)

07 animations + 10 threat layer + 12 split presents. Best return for teaching. Pairs with A. Requires stable keyed tokens in the board component.

C. The big bet (L)

05 spacetime cube as a secondary view (or 04 isometric), with 03 worldlines as the 2D fallback. Spectacular for replay (15) and sharing, risky as the primary play surface.

D. Reach (M)

11 mobile + 13 setup and lobby + 14 onboarding. Nothing about the board changes; more people get to a first turn.

Where the files are

All pages live in mockups/ and open from file:// with no build. Shared tokens are in shared/mock.css, the fixture in shared/fixture.js (regenerate with node --import tsx mockups/shared/make-fixture.ts), the baseline board renderer in shared/board.js, and current-prototype screenshots in current/.
