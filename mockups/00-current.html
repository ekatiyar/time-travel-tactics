Baseline: the prototype today

Screenshots of the current play screen captured by driving the real build with a stubbed peer, with the problems each later page tries to solve. Bootstrap mode, 16x9, seed "demo", two players.

Captured by mockups/current/capture.mjsDark theme unless noted

What works

  • One combined board for present and history, as the design doc asks. The focus turn and look-back sliders do exactly what they say.
  • Filled vs hollow bodies for "your direction vs opposite" is a strong, cheap encoding, and it is reused on the strip and trail dots.
  • Commit state is always visible. Keyboard flow (WASD, Enter, Esc) is complete.
  • The event log is honest and complete. Every rule outcome is a sentence.

Problems the other pages address

  1. The board gets about half the width and 70vh of height on a laptop. The log and the turn card, which change rarely, get the rest.01, 11
  2. History is corner dots. At 16x9 a trail dot is 11% of a cell, roughly 4px. Direction of travel through world time is not visible at all; you cannot tell which way a player walked.03, 04, 05
  3. The strip is three 7px rows of dots and triangles. Playheads for four players overlap in one column. The horizon is a dashed box.02, 12
  4. The front is a small triangle and the ETA lives in hover text. The doc's requirement to show the personal-index gap is met only on hover.02, 06, 07, 10
  5. The key is a small square on a body side. Nothing shows the steal tile beyond it, or who will hold a real key after the front lands.06, 10
  6. Legality only through disabled buttons. The reason ("Blocked by wall") is a tooltip on a disabled control, which most users never see; on the board a blocked target tile is grey with a title attribute.08, 09
  7. Every body carries its index in a 26cqmin font. Stacks show "16 … 18". This is noisy on busy boards and unreadable under about 28px cells.17
  8. Turns resolve with a re-render. Bounces, grabs, inversions, and front steps all happen invisibly between two frames.07
  9. The legend is ten always-on items under the board, and the priority card is separate from the controls it affects.01, 08
  10. Setup is a seven-field form with no picture of the board. The lobby is a list of colour rows with no relation to spawns.13
  11. No teaching. A single hint sentence ("Grab the key from beside the center…") is the only in-game rule help. Inversion is a button labelled "Turn around in time".14
  12. Touch cannot inspect. All body details are title attributes.09, 11
  13. After the match the screen says "Use World turn to review the match". There is no replay over meta-turns, no summary, nothing to share.15
  14. Mobile stacks the three columns. The board comes first, then sliders, then controls below the fold; committing needs a scroll every turn.11
  15. Hold is pre-selected. The app falls back to hold (or invert) so Commit always has a legal target, but the highlighted button reads as "you already chose". 08
  16. "World turn" and "turn" sit side by side with similar names: one is the scrub position, the other the meta-turn out of the cap.02, 12
  17. Inverting shows nothing on the board. The token stays put; only the log line and the header word "backward" change.07, 03
